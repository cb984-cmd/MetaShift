## Scientific position

The contribution is **MetaShift-Bench**, a target-fixed benchmark for
information-constrained selective scope auditing. A reported AQS Method Code
transition is an event anchor, not a physical-instrument log, ground-truth bias
label, or causal intervention. The v0.5 target-fixed pairs measure a finite
policy's answerable coverage under a stipulated synthetic comparison channel;
they do not identify a real mechanism. MetaShift remains a tested
metadata-informed weighting hypothesis, and neither MetaShift variant has a
confidence-supported overall advantage over standard synthetic control.
