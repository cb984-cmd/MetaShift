\subsection{AQS audit protocol}

For each primary-parameter anchor $a=(s,p,t_0,m^-,m^+)$, the audit:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
  \item validates canonical daily observations and identifies a persistent
  reported Method Code transition;
  \item constructs a geographic candidate pool at distinct physical sites,
  retaining at most one POC per site by the pre-event ranking rule;
  \item records donor insufficiency if fewer than three qualified physical
  donors exist;
  \item fits cross-site weights and the residual center only on the inclusive
  $t_0-180$ through $t_0-15$ calibration slice;
  \item requires at least two available donors and 30 retained observations in
  each required residual window;
  \item records an input-window failure if those calculation conditions fail;
  \item computes diagnostic intervals, placebos, donor sensitivity, and
  protocol evidence tiers for complete cases; and
  \item preserves complete cases, failures, and unavailable diagnostics.
\end{enumerate}
This is an implementation specification for a metadata-anchored audit; it does
not identify a physical mechanism.

\paragraph{Deployment residual.}
For a qualified target $i=(s,p)$ and donors $j\in N_a$, the AQS audit uses
\[
  r_{i,t}=z_{i,t}-\sum_{j\in N_a}w_{ij}z_{j,t}
  -\operatorname{median}_{u\in T_{\mathrm{cal}}}
  \left(z_{i,u}-\sum_{j\in N_a}w_{ij}z_{j,u}\right).
\]
Its displayed effect is the post-minus-pre median residual on the log scale.
It is a diagnostic local residual discontinuity, not a causal physical-bias
estimate or a corrected concentration.
