# Formal-report human completion checklist

**Status: HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED.** This checklist is a handoff template. It
does not assert that any review, signature, disclosure, or taxonomy decision
has happened.

## Evidence and scientific boundaries

- [ ] Every student author can explain the distinct-physical-donor rule and why
  older POC-level donor results are superseded.
- [ ] Every student author can explain why the frozen synthetic result does not
  establish an overall MetaShift advantage over standard synthetic control.
- [ ] Every student author can explain why a Method Code transition is an event
  anchor rather than proof of an instrument replacement, failure, or bias.
- [ ] Every student author can explain why real-event interval results are
  diagnostic and not calibrated 95% confidence intervals.
- [ ] Student author(s) and supervising teacher have independently reviewed the
  final `v0.3.2-evidence-final` evidence release and the final PDF.

## Taxonomy and factual review

- [ ] Student author(s) and supervising teacher independently complete the
  row-by-row Method Code taxonomy review before any taxonomy-stratified result
  is added.
- [ ] The final paper contains no taxonomy-stratified result until that review
  is documented.
- [ ] Student author(s) verify every identity, school, advisor, date, and
  factual statement that replaces a visible placeholder.
- [ ] Student author(s) verify every citation and reference against the source
  actually consulted.

## Contributions and disclosures

- [ ] Each student author verifies the 500--1500 word topic, data, analysis,
  writing, and contribution account in the closing declaration.
- [ ] Each supervising teacher or advisor verifies the accurate description of
  their institution, permitted role, guidance, and any compensation.
- [ ] Student author(s) complete the AI-use record using
  `docs/AI_ASSISTANCE_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md`; no assistance is omitted or invented.
- [ ] Student author(s) verify that the report and submission package contain
  no credentials, raw API responses, private information, or unapproved data.

## Official submission materials

- [ ] All student authors and supervising teachers sign the academic-integrity
  declaration and any required competition forms.
- [ ] Required school and outside-institution stamps are obtained.
- [ ] A final plagiarism report is generated for the exact submitted PDF.
- [ ] Student author(s) and supervising teacher make the final truthfulness
  attestation after reviewing the exact final PDF.
