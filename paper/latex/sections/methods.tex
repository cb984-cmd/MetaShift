\section{Methods}

\subsection{Counterfactual residual}

For target monitor $i$, date $t$, and selected geographic donors $j \in N_i$,
the daily concentration $y_{i,t}$ is transformed as
\[
z_{i,t} = \log(1 + \max(y_{i,t}, 0)).
\]
For a donor-weighted counterfactual, the residual is
\[
r_{i,t} = z_{i,t} - \sum_{j \in N_i} w_{ij}z_{j,t}
- \operatorname{median}_{s \in T_{\mathrm{cal}}}
\left(z_{i,s} - \sum_{j \in N_i}w_{ij}z_{j,s}\right).
\]
Weights are nonnegative and sum to one. They are fitted using only the
pre-anchor calibration interval, ending before a buffer immediately preceding
the anchor. The primary effect is the difference between post- and pre-anchor
median residuals in symmetric windows. Raw-unit effects are retained as an
interpretation aid, not as interchangeable causal estimands.

Fixed-prior MetaShift uses a reliability preference based on pre-event
correlation and distance. Cross-validated MetaShift selects among frozen
pre-event settings only. Standard synthetic control fits its donor weights
without the MetaShift reliability prior; nearest-neighbor difference-in-
differences uses the closest qualified donor reference. Before-after median,
Bayesian mean shift, CUSUM, rolling-MAD, and PELT provide single-series
comparators.

\subsection{Evidence diagnostics}

The real-event audit combines several diagnostics without assigning a physical
cause:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item pre-event counterfactual fit;
  \item fixed-weight moving-block intervals;
  \item selection-aware nested moving-block intervals that re-evaluate donor
  correlation eligibility, donor selection, and weight fitting inside the
  observed candidate pool;
  \item stable post-transition time placebos and exploratory
  Benjamini--Hochberg-adjusted screening quantities;
  \item donor-as-treated and date-resampling placebos; and
  \item leave-one-donor-out refits.
\end{enumerate}

An anchor is labelled a supported candidate discontinuity only when all
predeclared available evidence conditions hold. An event with available but
failed conditions is not supported by the available evidence; an event without
a necessary comparative or diagnostic input is inconclusive. These are
observational audit tiers. They are not labels for instrument failures,
confirmed bias, or true pollution corrections.

\subsection{Inference boundary}

The nested procedure captures some time-resampling, donor-reselection, and
weight-refitting variation within the observed candidate pool. It does not
capture uncertainty in source metadata, site geography, or initial candidate-
pool construction. Accordingly, even when an interval excludes zero it is a
diagnostic statement about the protocol-defined residual, not a calibrated
confidence interval for a physical measurement bias.
